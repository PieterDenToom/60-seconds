How to Play

1
Divide into teams of at least 2 people each.
2
Explain the words shown to your team. You can only use one-syllable words.
3
Navigate words using the "Next" and "Previous" buttons. You cannot skip words.
4
If you make a mistake (say part of the word, use multi-syllable words, etc.), you lose the round and must press "End Round".
5
Score points for each word you successfully explain to your team within the time limit.
